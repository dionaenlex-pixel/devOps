Fix a route

Pick two places to see the route. If part of the line is wrong, click that part and fix it on the map.

Drag any white dot on the line to move it, or click a step to fix that part. The round pins (places and walkway junctions) can be dragged too, and they save straight away.

    • Drag a white dot to move the line.
    • Click the map to add a bend where the line should go.
    • Double-click a dot to remove it.
